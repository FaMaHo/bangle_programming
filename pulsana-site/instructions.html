Set up your device

Follow these steps in order the first time.

Full video walkthrough Coming soon

Remember: 48 hours, worn continuously

The study needs an unbroken 48-hour recording. The Bangle.js 2 is not water-protected — take it off before showering.

1

Install the app and enter your code

Download PulseWatch AI, open it, choose “I have an enrollment code,” and paste the code from the website.

Screenshot: website
Screenshot: app
2

Find your bracelet

Tap the device icon at the bottom of the screen, then tap “Scan for device.”

Screenshot: device icon
Screenshot: scanning
3

Connect to your bracelet

When it appears in the list, tap it to connect. It should be listed as —

[ Bluetooth name to be filled in — e.g. Bangle.js xxxx ]
Screenshot: device list
Screenshot: connected
4

Get to know your Bangle.js 2

A few things that make the watch easier to use from the first minute:

  • Hold the button for ~2 seconds to jump back to the clock face.
  • A lock icon top-left means the touchscreen is off; press the button to unlock.
  • From the clock face, press the button to open the App Launcher, then drag to scroll and tap to open.
  • In text menus, drag to scroll and tap to select; in number menus, drag to change the value and tap to confirm.
5

Open PulseWatch AI on the watch and start recording

Find PulseWatch AI in the App Launcher, open it, then tap the “Record” box to begin.

Screenshot: app list on watch
Screenshot: Record screen

Remember: 48 hours, worn continuously

Keep it on day and night, off only for showers.

6

Charging

The magnetic charging cable plugs into any USB port and only carries power, no data. Don't force it if it doesn't seat easily, and don't leave it plugged in unattended.
