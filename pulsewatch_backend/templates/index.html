Cardiac research · FILS, POLITEHNICA Bucharest

Catch heart sclerosis years before it has symptoms.

Pulsana is a research study built around a small wrist bracelet and an AI model that watches your heart rate variability while you go about your day.

Join the study See how it works
What you'll be using

Three parts, one pipeline

01

The bracelet

A Bangle.js 2 smartwatch, worn on the wrist.

02

The PulseWatch AI app

Your phone pulls the readings off the bracelet over Bluetooth.

03

The AI analysis

An XGBoost model looks for early autonomic patterns.

Why this matters

Built for the years before a check-up would catch anything

You can't reasonably repeat an MRI or ECG every week — and most people only book one once something already feels wrong.

The usual path

  • Tests like echocardiography, MRI, and biomarker panels are accurate, but require a hospital visit.
  • Most people book that visit once symptoms already appear.

The Pulsana path

  • A regular bracelet quietly tracks your pulse and movement in the background — no appointment needed.
  • It watches continuously, so it can flag early changes while you still feel completely fine.
Data & privacy

Your heartbeat stays yours

Your report is generated on your phone.

On-device & local

Bracelet → phone → report, generated locally every time. Sharing with the research server is separate, and always under your control.
